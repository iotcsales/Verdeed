BEFORE YOU BEGIN

This is an AI document review aid — not legal advice

Verdeed Document Intelligence uses AI to read, classify, draft, and compare real estate documents. Please understand what that means before you rely on it:

  • It can misread, mis-extract, or misinterpret information — always verify facts against the original document and official records.
  • Drafted documents are first-draft starting points only, with placeholders for missing details. They must be reviewed by a licensed advocate before signing, stamping, or registering anything.
  • It does not verify documents against government registries, and it cannot detect forged signatures, stamps, or seals.
  • Your Anthropic API key stays in your browser for this session only and is sent only to Anthropic's API — never stored or transmitted anywhere else by this tool.
