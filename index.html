BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A quick word before you begin

Verdeed is a powerful AI tool that helps you read, draft, and compare real estate documents — genuinely useful for a first pass, but worth knowing how to use it well:

  • It's a great starting point, but always double-check the details that matter against the original document and official records.
  • Drafts are meant as a first version to work from, with placeholders for anything missing. Have a lawyer review the final version before you sign, stamp, or register anything.
  • It works from what you give it — it doesn't independently check government registries or verify signatures, stamps, or seals, so those are still worth confirming separately.
  • Your API key stays right in your browser for this session — it's only ever sent to Anthropic's API, never stored or shared anywhere else by this tool.
